Emerson Electric is a Fortune 500 global automation leader with more than 135 years of innovation history. Its portfolio spans automation systems, discrete automation, final control, measurement instrumentation, and test and measurement, marketed through brands such as Fisher, Rosemount, DeltaV, Micro Motion, ASCO, Bettis, PACSystems, Flexim, and NI. Emerson serves industries including power, oil and gas, chemical, life sciences, food and beverage, automotive, data centers, mining, and marine. This repository captures the public APIs, developer resources, and machine-readable artifacts associated with Emerson Electric.
